## Owner decisions before institutional or paid launch

Policy freshness has no agreed interval. Valid old dates still pass the date
integrity check; do not call that freshness verification. Choose a canonical
policy before seeding permission-granting facts:

1. A common maximum verification age: simpler operations, with potentially
   unnecessary blocking for stable policies and late discovery of fast changes.
2. Per-source validity/review dates maintained by human reviewers: more precise,
   requiring a reviewed pack contract and responsibility for timely re-checks.
3. Keep generation blocked until a reviewed pilot has explicit validity and
   program/course applicability: smaller initial release, strongest evidence.

Program/course facts cannot unlock unit-wide capabilities because callers do
not establish the narrower enrollment context. Define the identity/applicability
contract before enabling them. An invalid or narrower first matching fact
currently blocks rather than falling back to a broader permission.

For premium launch scope, release only workflows with authenticated evidence,
or keep the affected premium offering unavailable until its promised workflow
is proved. No paid feature was activated to make a marketing claim true.
